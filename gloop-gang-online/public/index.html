How to win

①
Play your cards

Cards cost purple GOO, which refills over time. Drag a card onto your half of the arena or tap a card then choose a spot.

②
Fight in two lanes

Troops automatically attack enemies and march towards the opposing towers. Put tough units in front and ranged units behind.

③
Own the rifts

Hold the two glowing nodes in the middle with your troops. Each one you control makes your GOO refill faster.

④
Destroy their core

Take out a side tower to open a route to the core. Destroy the core to win instantly, or have more surviving towers when time expires.

⑤
Build a stronger squad

Win trophies to unlock new units, customise your eight-card deck, and upgrade your favourites with coins.
